<h2 align="left">What I'm Building</h2>

<h3>AION — AI Operations Network</h3>

<p>
I'm building <b>AION</b>, an AI-powered e-commerce automation platform designed
to help businesses automate customer conversations, sales, marketing, and
day-to-day operations across channels such as WhatsApp and Instagram.
</p>

<p>
AION brings customer conversations, commerce, AI agents, marketing,
workflows, and business intelligence into a unified platform.
</p>

<ul>
<li>AI-powered customer support and sales automation</li>
<li>Unified WhatsApp & Instagram conversations</li>
<li>E-commerce and order automation</li>
<li>AI business insights and contextual customer understanding</li>
<li>Workflow automation and AI agents</li>
<li>Business analytics and operational reporting</li>
<li>Integrations with platforms such as Shopify and Meta</li>
</ul>

<p>
<b>Built from the ground up with:</b>
NestJS, React, Python, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis, Docker,
Meta Graph API, LLMs, and RAG.
</p>
